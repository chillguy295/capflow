  • 5Under 1 hour old
  • 5.5Still on the bonding curve
  • 5.5Market cap under $50K
  • 6Ticker used 3+ times before
  • 6No socials
  • 7.5Under a day old

FAQ

What is CapFlow?

A bookmark that scores the Axiom coin you have open.

Does it need my wallet?

No. No wallet, login or extension.

Where does the data come from?

The Axiom page, DexScreener and RugCheck.

Why did a new coin score low?

Tiny and brand-new coins are capped, however clean they look. Under $10K market cap the most they can get is 3.5, marked “Too early”.

Is a high score a reason to buy?

No. It shows how a coin is set up, not where the price goes. Not financial advice.
